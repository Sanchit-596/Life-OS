## Brand & Style
The design system pairs executive focus with algorithmic intelligence. Built for life architecture, daily orchestration, and high-stakes personal productivity, the aesthetic unifies Linear's tactical precision with ChatGPT’s ambient clarity. The visual language conveys quiet authority, absolute predictability, and spatial composure.

Key brand attributes:
- **Quiet Precision**: Restrained structural framing, razor-thin borders, and zero ornamental noise.
- **Ambient Intelligence**: Luminescent state indicators and translucent surface layers that signal computational awareness without distraction.
- **Cognitive Ease**: Deep obsidian canvases that eliminate eye fatigue during extensive planning and execution loops.
- **Tactile Legibility**: High-contrast typographic hierarchy paired with monospaced data fidelity for immediate comprehension.

## Colors
The system employs an obsidian slate foundation engineered specifically to mitigate display glare while preserving crisp separation across elevated panels. 

### Surface Architecture
- **Base Canvas**: `#0a0d12` serves as the primary unlit canvas.
- **Surface Elevation 1**: `#0f141c` acts as the standard panel, board, and card fill.
- **Surface Elevation 2**: `#161c26` handles popovers, floating command inputs, and active drawers.
- **Structural Muted Borders**: `#1e2633` demarcates dividers, subtle cell borders, and card perimeters at 1px width.

### Intelligent Accents & Semantic States
- **AI Core & Engine**: `#6366f1` (Electric Indigo) denotes automated planning, intelligence triggers, and primary actions.
- **Insight & Diagnostics**: `#06b6d4` (Lucid Cyan) flags replan suggestions, optimizations, and synthesis states.
- **Feasibility & Flow**: `#10b981` (Subtle Emerald) signals balanced workloads, completed milestones, and high-confidence estimates.
- **Precaution & Saturation**: `#f59e0b` (Amber) indicates near-capacity schedules, loose constraints, and non-blocking warnings.
- **Conflict & Deficit**: `#f43f5e` (Rose/Crimson) highlights over-commitments, deadlocks, and urgent timeline dependencies.

### Text Contrast
- **Text High**: `#f1f5f9` (95% contrast) for primary data and headings.
- **Text Muted**: `#94a3b8` for descriptors, field labels, and secondary context.
- **Text Subtle**: `#475569` for empty states, hotkey hints, and disabled tracks.

## Typography
Typographic rhythm relies on a dual-engine architecture:
- **Inter** handles narrative clarity, system interfaces, task titles, and conversational AI interactions. Strict negative tracking on large scales ensures tight, editorial quality.
- **JetBrains Mono** manages time allocation tokens, duration values, schedule slots, numeric KPIs, and shortcut indicators. Its mechanical geometry locks information into clean, tabular visual planes.

All metrics, countdown timers, and operational statistics must render exclusively via `JetBrains Mono` with tabular figures enabled (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent horizontal jitter during real-time updates.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to a flexible 12-column structural grid on desktop views that reflows into a single-column, bottom-anchored canvas on mobile screens.

### Grid & Viewport Behavior
- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12-column fluid grid framed within a max width of 1600px. Persistent modular sidebar (240px to 280px fixed width) paired with a responsive primary workspace, supplemented by a collapsable contextual inspector or AI drawer (360px).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout. The contextual panel shifts to an elevated slide-over sheet. Gutters scale down to 1rem.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px)**: Single-column stacked stream. Sidebars convert into a swipe-activated sheet or quick-access bottom tray. Canvas margins tighten to 1rem.

### Rhythm
Components apply an 8px base rhythm, utilizing `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) for internal input/badge compaction, and `space-lg` (20px) through `space-xl` (32px) to clearly segment isolated analytical sections.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is created through subtle luminance tiers, precise hairline borders, and frosted translucent overlays. Heavy drop shadows are omitted in favor of localized luminous diffusions.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Base)**: `#0a0d12` solid foundation.
- **Tier 1 (Panels & Cards)**: `#0f141c` filled with 1px solid `#1e2633` perimeter.
- **Tier 2 (Overlays & Menus)**: `rgba(22, 28, 38, 0.85)` surface supplemented by `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a subtle ambient edge highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`).
- **Tier 3 (Floating Command Palette / Modals)**: `rgba(15, 20, 28, 0.95)` surface surrounded by a 1px border of `#2a3447` and a soft, low-intensity ambient aura: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.

### Radiant Glow Anchors
Interactive active states (such as an ongoing focus sprint, active replan computation, or critical deficit) employ faint peripheral glows via `box-shadow: 0 0 20px -5px [accent_color_alpha_20]`.

## Shapes
Surfaces feature smooth, refined corners that balance ergonomic friendliness with utilitarian density.

- **Primary Cards & Modals**: `1rem` (16px) border radius to establish distinct, calm zones.
- **Interactive Controls (Inputs, Action Buttons, Badges)**: `0.5rem` (8px) border radius for compact, uniform targets.
- **Key Caps & Status Dots**: `0.25rem` (4px) for inline keycaps; fully rounded (`9999px`) for semantic status indicators and micro-avatars.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (AI / Replan)**: Gradient-tinted background using `#6366f1` transitioning subtly to `#4f46e5`, white text, 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`. On hover, an ambient indigo bloom (`0 0 12px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.35)`) activates.
- **Secondary**: `#161c26` background, `#f1f5f9` text, 1px solid border in `#1e2633`. Hover shifts fill to `#1e2633`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary**: Transparent background, `#94a3b8` text. Hover yields `#0f141c` fill and `#f1f5f9` text.
- **Sizes**: Standard action height of 36px (padding: 0 14px) with typography set to `body-md` font weight 500.

### Chips & Status Indicators
- Constructed at 22px height with `0.25rem` (4px) or pill radius.
- Composed with transparent tinted fills (10% opacity of state color), matching border (20% opacity), and bright foreground text:
  - **Feasible**: Fill `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.10)`, Border `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25)`, Text `#10b981`.
  - **Warning**: Fill `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.10)`, Border `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)`, Text `#f59e0b`.
  - **Conflict**: Fill `rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.10)`, Border `rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.25)`, Text `#f43f5e`.
  - **AI Synthesized**: Fill `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.10)`, Border `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.25)`, Text `#06b6d4`.

### Cards & Blocks
- Base container: `#0f141c` fill, 1px border in `#1e2633`, radius `1rem`.
- Padding set to `1.25rem` (20px).
- Internal block separation utilizes `1px solid #161c26` dividers.

### Form Inputs & Search Fields
- Inactive state: `#0a0d12` surface, 1px solid `#1e2633`, `#f1f5f9` text, `#475569` placeholder.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#6366f1` with an inner ring glow (`0 0 0 1px #6366f1`).
- Integrated keyboard hint badges: Right-aligned keycaps (`label-kbd`) encased in `#161c26` with `#94a3b8` text and 1px border in `#1e2633`.

### Interactive Lists & Feed Rows
- Borderless rows resting inside container cards. Separated by hairline borders (`#161c26`).
- Hover state: Background switches to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)`.
- Left-edge accent bars (2px wide) appear dynamically on active or selected items using `#6366f1` or the semantic status color.

### Checkboxes & Binary Selectors
- Unchecked: 16px square, `#0a0d12` background, 1px solid `#2a3447`, rounded `4px`.
- Checked: `#6366f1` solid fill, white checkmark glyph, `box-shadow: 0 0 8px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.4)`.

### Command Palette (Keyboard-First HUD)
- Centered modal anchored at 20% viewport top, 640px wide.
- High-blur translucent base: `rgba(15, 20, 28, 0.94)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`.
- Header houses a borderless input field running `headline-sm` typography with an ambient search glyph and instant-query execution.
- Footer displays keyboard navigation hints: `↑↓` to navigate, `↵` to execute, `ESC` to dismiss, formatted in `label-kbd`.
